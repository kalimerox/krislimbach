About

Kris Limbach (born 1978) is an audiovisual artist and film maker based in Berlin. He comes to audiovisual art from experimental sound, and he is just as involved in film, constantly exploring the formats and aesthetics of cinema. He studied applied physics at Freie Universität Berlin, and that knowledge runs through his sound and image work. His first experimental film, Cuento Incompleto, was published in 2005 in a book project in Madrid.

In his sound work he uses film editing techniques, prepared drums, tape manipulation, no input mixing and a vast amount of raw and processed field recordings. He has released on labels such as Richard Garet’s Contour Editions, Staaltape, Modisti, Framework Radio and Agxivatein, and has worked with John Bock, Pierce Warnecke, Hopek Quirin, Jochen Arbeit, Rinus van Alebeek, Rieko Okuda, Juan Antonio Nieto (Pangea), Lisa Müller-Trede and many others.

His experimental feature films Music for Dysfunctional Airports and The Lost Color were shown internationally at film and media art festivals. His film work moves between highly abstract live film and video manipulation and feature-like experimental cinema. Years of set recording, composition and scoring, especially for the performance artist and film maker John Bock, shaped his process based approach to film making. He has just finished his third feature, Ohnmacht, premiering in winter 2026.

Curating

Together with Pierce Warnecke and Seiji Morimoto he runs the emitter micro label and the emitter micro festival, a biennial festival of experimental sound in Berlin. He curates evenings of experimental music and performance in many places, from an emitter night at the Kontakte festival at Akademie der Künste to T.A.T.V., Temporary Autonomous Television, the expanded television festival he directs.

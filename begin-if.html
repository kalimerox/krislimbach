begin _if_ ()

Series of sound works · since 2005

A long term series based on field recordings, first conceived in Madrid in 2005 and brought to life in Berlin in 2009. It joins field recordings and studio work, cutting techniques from visual design and film post production, concrete sounds and improvisation. Each edition responds to its own medium, following a concept inspired by hidden variable theories that stays hidden to the listener.

  • begin _if_ (4)2023 · Contour Editions
  • begin _if_ (3)2014 · cassette, Agxivatein · the tape itself as material: hiss and saturation, field recordings from Tokyo and Berlin, vinyl and tape manipulation · review in The Sound Projector
  • begin _if_ (2)2010 · modisti and framework:afield · with Sarah Pitsulala Reaburn and Bajba, vocals
  • begin _if_ (1)2009 · Contour Editions · with Luisa Kraft, flute

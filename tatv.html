T.A.T.V.

Temporary Autonomous Tele Vision · expanded television festival · founder · since 2020

T.A.T.V. treats television as an experimental format built around live sound and image: a small autonomous republic that broadcasts concerts, performances, radio, installations and talks. Everything is filmed and edited live over three days of transmission, and the backstage melts into the stage. Six editions since 2020, publicly funded by Musikfonds and Initiative Neue Musik Berlin.

  • T.A.T.V. 629 to 31 May 2026 · Galiläakirche, Berlin · Musikfonds · John Bock, Liz Kosack, Kris Limbach, JD Zazie, Ployz, Teresa Riemann, Jasmine Guffond, Maria Camila Agudelo, Niko de Paula Lefort, with an evening in memory of Hopek Quirin
  • T.A.T.V. 516 September 2023 · Museum Kesselhaus Herzberge, Berlin, as part of Zusammenkünste · nine hours of live transmission · Mizuki Ishikawa, Frank Bretschneider, Corazón de Robota, Zustand D.
  • T.A.T.V. 3.512 June 2022 · Liebig12, Berlin · Jeff Surak, DuChamp, Liz Kosack, Munsha, Kris Limbach

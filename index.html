Kris Limbach is a Berlin based sound artist and composer. He builds non linear structures in time out of field recordings, tape, prepared drums, electronics and improvisation, using cutting techniques from film post production. Film comes into his work from the sound side: scores for John Bock, live performances for film and tape, and his own experimental features.

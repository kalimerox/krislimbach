emitter micro

Label & festival · Berlin · since 2011

A label and biennial festival of experimental sound, curated by Kris Limbach and Pierce Warnecke and later joined by Seiji Morimoto. The releases are distributed through Staalplaat.

emitter micro 2015 three days at Taborkirche, Ausland and NK with Jacob Kirkegaard, aspec(t), Marta Zapparoli, Frank Bretschneider, Andy Graydon, Kasia Justka, Jim Haynes, Hanzmemorial and Jd Zazie
Editions 2011, 2015, 2017, 2019, 2023

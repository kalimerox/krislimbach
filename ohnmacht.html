Ohnmacht

Experimental feature film · 2026

Civilization is coming to an end. Technology has not kept its promise to solve the problems of the world and has left behind a toxic wasteland, a planet ruled by malevolent forces.

May is a singer songwriter early in her career. Between recording, touring, publicity dates and press meetings she finds herself trapped in a “making of” world. On her journey she becomes a key figure of the Ohnmacht resistance, a clandestine group of women fighting with their limited resources to secure their survival and legacy.

Kris Limbach’s third experimental feature film, as director, editor and sound designer. As in all his films, the editing follows a musical logic as much as a cinematic one.

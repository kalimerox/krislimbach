Vertical Movements

Compositional research · ambisonics, installation, live

Mountains, hills, castles, lighthouses and skyscrapers: elevations, natural or man made, play an important role in human life. The ascent, climbing a mountain or taking a lift to the 40th floor, is carved into individual and collective memory. Vertical Movements is a piece about height and verticality, with vertigo as a sensorial state between anxiety and enlightenment.

Sounds are composed in space rather than in time, with simultaneous vertical realities colliding. Field recordings made on hills and heights across Europe and beyond, electronics and acoustic instruments are mixed in higher order ambisonics, so upper and lower movements are heard separately and the listener can focus on different regions. Psychoacoustics, like Blauert’s directional bands, are part of the score. The final presentations are hybrids of sound installation, live performance and a vinyl release.

Supported by the Musikfonds scholarship programme within Neustart Kultur 2020/2021. Thanks to Petra Goldman and Cultur im Corbusierhaus, Bradley Schirmer and EMS Stockholm.

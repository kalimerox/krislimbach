Sonic Landscape Interventions

Site-specific sound walk · Sonoma County, California

A soundscape made for the Warnecke Ranch in Sonoma County. With a phone and headphones, walkers hear field recordings, radio pieces and interviews (re)recorded on site, and build their own narrative through the path they choose, which is tracked and published online. The project also exists as a sound box multiple: 20 signed boxes, each with a CD, a booklet and a different found object.

The walk online Listen: ¡RE-record!

To walk it, install Miniatures for Mobiles (iOS, Android) and search for Sonic Landscape Interventions.
